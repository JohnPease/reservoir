Four foldable concepts

The first set only rearranged the existing cards. These start from what each screen is good at. Every concept has a cover version and an inner version, and the figures are examples. Nothing here is built.

A

The tank

The app is named for a reservoir, so today is drawn as one. The water level is what is left to spend; every purchase lowers it a step you can read down the side. On the cover it is the whole screen, and unfolding makes it tall with the day's steps written beside it.

B

The month strip

The daily limit with carryover is the idea no other budget app has, and nothing in the app shows how the carryover is built. This puts every day of the month in one row. Bars over the base turn red, and the line below shows what each day added to or took from tomorrow.

C

Now and next

The fold gives the inner display a natural top and bottom. The top is what you spend from today, the bottom is what is coming, so a screen that mixes both today becomes two clear zones. The cover keeps only the top and a one-line preview of the bottom.

D

The ledger spread

Categories, trends and unusual-charge flags are the newest part of the app and have the most detail. On the inner display they become a book: summary on the left page, the charges behind a row on the right, with no navigation push.

Decision for you

Pick one to prototype, or tell me which pieces to combine. My suggestion: B on the inner display for budget history, A as the cover glance, and D for Transactions.
