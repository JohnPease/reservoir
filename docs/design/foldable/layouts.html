Today, four ways

Rules behind the layouts

Switch on width, not on deviceUse size classes and a width threshold of about 600 pt. Anything wider than that gets the two-pane layout, which also covers iPad later.
Hero first, then the supporting cardsThe daily limit stays top-left in every layout. Cards keep their current order, filled across columns.
Swap the tab bar for a rail in wide landscapeA bottom bar costs 56 pt of a 466 pt tall screen. A 132 pt rail gives that height back.
Fold-safe stateFolding or unfolding rebuilds the window. Scroll position, open sheets and the selected tab must survive it.

Which screens come first? I'd start with Today across all four layouts, because the others reuse the same container. Then Transactions (list and detail side by side on the inner display) and Budget.
